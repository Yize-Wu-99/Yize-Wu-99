## EDR Viewer for Mac

I develop EDR Viewer, an electrophysiology recording viewer for Apple Silicon Macs.

Open **WinEDR EDR**, **pCLAMP / Axon ABF and ATF**, and regularly sampled **TXT, CSV and TSV** data on macOS.

- **Free Viewer Mode:** inspect traces and make A/B measurements.
- **Optional paid Full Access:** processing, FFT/PSD, exports and Analysis.
- **Requirements:** Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later.

Looking for **WinEDR for Mac**, **pCLAMP for Mac** or a way to view **Clampfit recordings on Mac**? See the supported workflows and limitations below.

**[Download EDR Viewer and read the guides →](https://github.com/Yize-Wu-99/edr-viewer-releases)**

EDR Viewer is an independent application, not an official Mac version of WinEDR, pCLAMP or Clampfit.
